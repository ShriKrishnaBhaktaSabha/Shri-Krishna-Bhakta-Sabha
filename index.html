Our approach

End-to-end bhakti, the traditional way

श्रवणं कीर्तनं विष्णोः स्मरणं पादसेवनम् ।
अर्चनं वन्दनं दास्यं सख्यमात्मनिवेदनम् ॥
— Shrimad Bhagavatam, on the nine forms of bhakti (Navadha Bhakti)

These verses describe bhakti as something lived through the whole person — hearing, chanting, remembering, and serving. Our weekly programs are built around four of these limbs.

Listen to the katha
Kirtana

Our story

Founded on the simple wish to chant, learn, and serve together

Shri Krishna Bhakta Sabha began as a small group of neighbours gathering for evening bhajan and grew, year by year, into a community that welcomes anyone drawn to the path of bhakti.

Every gathering is offered free of cost and open to the public — children, elders, and newcomers to the tradition are equally welcome.

Our satsang draws on the Bhagavad Gita, the Srimad Bhagavatam, and the songs of the Vaishnava saints, and is guided by a small circle of volunteer Acharyas and speakers who give their time freely.

Beyond weekly gatherings, the Sabha runs a full festival calendar through the year, and a small seva team that looks after prasadam, upkeep, and outreach.
